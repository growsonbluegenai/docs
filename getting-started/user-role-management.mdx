A project has one owner and any number of people invited to it, each holding a role that decides what they can do.

## The Three Roles

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The person who created the project. Manages collaborators and their roles |
| **Editor** | Can work in the project: prompt the agents, edit the CAB, change Tickets |
| **Viewer** | Can see the project without changing it |

**Owner is not something you are granted.** It comes from creating the project.

## Inviting Someone

<Frame caption="Manage Collaboration. Invitations take a role, and the members list shows who already has one.">
  ![The Manage Collaboration drawer showing an Invite Collaborators card with an email field and Viewer and Editor permission options, a Shareable Link Access toggle, and a members list.](/images/getting-started/user-role-management-collaboration.png)
</Frame>

Open **Manage Collaborators** from the panel rail, or **Manage Collaborations** from a project card on the dashboard. Both open the same drawer.

Enter one or more email addresses, choose **Viewer** or **Editor**, and send. Invitations appear in the recipient's **Invitations** tab on their dashboard, and in your **Pending Invites** list until they respond.

<Frame caption="The members list separates people who have accepted from invitations still outstanding.">
  ![The members list showing tabs for Active Members and Pending Invites, a search field, and rows showing each member's name, email, date added, and role.](/images/getting-started/user-role-management-members.png)
</Frame>

An invitation can be accepted, rejected, or withdrawn before it is accepted. Someone who has accepted can later leave, or be removed.

<Note>
  Each collaborator carries a billing preference, set when they are invited: their agent runs are charged either to the project owner or to the collaborator.
</Note>

## Two Kinds of Sharing

These are different mechanisms with different audiences, and it is worth not confusing them.

**Shareable Link Access** is a toggle in the collaboration drawer. It lets anyone who is signed in and has the link **request** access to the project. It does not grant access; someone still approves the request.

**Read-only share links** are set per item, on things like an artifact or a finished CAB. Anyone with the link can open that one item **without signing in at all**. Nothing else in the project becomes visible.

<Warning>
  A read-only share link needs no account and no approval. Only enable one on an item you are content for anyone holding the URL to read.
</Warning>

## What a Role Changes

Roles are checked wherever an action would alter the project. A Viewer can open the workspace, read the CAB, and look at Tickets, but the controls that change things are unavailable to them.

Read-only questions are treated separately from changes, so a collaborator may be able to ask the agent about a codebase on a project where they cannot build.
